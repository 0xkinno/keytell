## 1. The registered test

**The data did not support the claim.** Over 94 out-of-sample weeks, each signal was added to an AR(2) forecast of weekly USDC volume at the lag chosen on in-sample weeks only. Gain is 1 minus the model's squared error over the baseline's: positive means smaller errors.

| Signal | In-sample lag (weeks) | In-sample gain | In-sample Spearman | Out-of-sample gain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| function-call-signed transactions | 4 | 5.6% | -0.36 | -5.1% |
| function-call share | 4 | 8.3% | -0.34 | -7.7% |
| daily active accounts | 4 | 3.7% | -0.20 | -3.6% |
| transaction count | 3 | 4.5% | 0.18 | 1.8% |
| NEAR price | 3 | 9.9% | 0.14 | -5.5% |

What happened: in-sample, function-call-signed activity at a 4-week lag lowered the error by 5.6%, but with a negative rank correlation (-0.36), so the fit was leaning on a relationship that ran the wrong way for a leading indicator. Out of sample the gain turned to -5.1%. Signals that lowered the out-of-sample error at all: transaction count. That is what a lag chosen on 58 noisy weeks looks like when it does not carry over.

Against DAA the mean squared-error difference is 0.006578 (95% interval -0.055139 to 0.069399); against price -0.001606 (-0.060525 to 0.058150). The pass rule needs both intervals below zero. Timing placebo: the real gain beats 9% of 1000 shifted series (the bar is 95%).

Files: `data/out/results.json`, `data/out/lags.csv`, `data/out/oos_errors.csv`, `data/out/placebo.csv`. Method: `docs/METHOD.md` (hash on chain).

## 2. Surprising finding: the signer moved, from app-held keys to relayers

The share of sampled transactions signed directly by a function-call key averaged 22.5% in 2022 and 6.4% in 2026, with a low of 1.6% in 2025. Meaning: the classic pattern, where an app holds a scoped key for its user and signs on its own, faded. NEP-366 meta transactions, where a relayer pays gas and the user's key signs the inner action, rose from 0.0% to a peak yearly average of 73.5% in 2025.

| Year | Weeks | Transactions classified | Function-call key | Meta transaction (relayed) | Implicit account | Full-access key | Function-call incl. relayed inner FC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 52 | 10,400 | 22.5% | 0.0% | 9.2% | 68.2% | 22.5% |
| 2023 | 52 | 10,400 | 19.1% | 0.4% | 21.2% | 59.2% | 19.2% |
| 2024 | 53 | 10,600 | 3.3% | 45.7% | 5.3% | 45.6% | 4.6% |
| 2025 | 52 | 10,400 | 1.6% | 73.5% | 1.0% | 23.9% | 2.5% |
| 2026 | 44 | 8,800 | 6.4% | 54.4% | 2.7% | 36.5% | 11.7% |

Yearly figures are means of weekly shares. Years with fewer sampled weeks are partial.

Why it matters for the thesis: the one control rerun that would pass the registered rule counts every relayed meta transaction as function-call activity (out-of-sample gain 0.1%, against -3.5% for DAA and -7.2% for price, over 97 weeks). This was picked after the fact out of 8 controls, so it is a hypothesis, not a result. It is the claim a next pre-registration should test: delegated signing, not the narrow function-call key, may be where NEAR's app usage now lives.

## 4. House of Stake names: keyless, and no delegation yet

House of Stake has leased 80 top-level names on mainnet (2026-09-18 to 2026-11-13, read from `registry.hos` at 2026-11-14T02:34 UTC). 80 of 80 hold no access key at all: they act only through their holder. 0 grant a wallet extension to any third party beyond the holder and House of Stake's default `ext.hos`, so delegated signing (the thing this thesis counts) has not reached them yet. A small side series, not part of the registered test. Rebuild: `npx tsx scripts/fetch-hos.ts` (free mainnet reads), rows in `data/raw/hos_names.csv`.

## The query

These findings come from the keyless block sample, not from a BigQuery scan (the full key ledger would scan about 20 TB). To reproduce one week from scratch and the yearly table from the committed sample:

```bash
make sample FROM=2025-01-06 TO=2025-01-06
```

```python
import pandas as pd
w = pd.read_csv('data/out/weekly.csv')
w = w[w.txs_classified > 0]
w['delegated'] = (w.n_delegated_inner_fc + w.n_delegated_inner_full) / w.txs_classified
print(w.groupby(w.monday.str[:4])[['fc_only_share', 'delegated']].mean())
```
